Schedule

Times are in {{.TZ}}. Each bar is a scheduled job, from its start time for as long as its backups take (never drawn narrower than a few pixels, and a name that doesn't fit in its bar is written beside it; the 5 and 15 minute zooms are for jobs that last seconds) (the median of its last 20, so one long first backup stops mattering after a run or two; dashed means no backup has finished yet and 15 minutes is assumed). Jobs on one agent run one after another, so overlapping bars in an agent lane wait for each other; jobs on one repository share its connections and bandwidth. Drag a bar sideways to change when its job starts (in 5 minute steps, or 1 minute in views of 3 hours or less); that works for a job with a single start time a day. Any other schedule is edited on the Jobs page. The striped bars are repository maintenance, drawn at its next due time for as long as its last cycles took (the median of 10); it holds the agent like a job does, and its due time shifts a little each day.
